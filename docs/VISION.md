# Vision

Living Room TV turns a Windows media PC into a calm, controller-oriented launch surface that feels closer to consumer electronics than a desktop shortcut collection.

The product should answer three questions immediately: what is available, where is focus, and what happens on confirmation. It prioritizes local operation, clear living-room typography, predictable navigation, and a native boundary small enough to understand and audit.

It is not an operating system, a streaming aggregator, a credential manager, or a cloud platform. It does not bypass service access controls or replace the terms and applications of configured services.
